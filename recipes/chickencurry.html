Description

Easy chicken curry with potatoes brings succulent chicken breast and spice-soaked potatoes together in a warming, Indian curry sauce. It’s honest, earthy and comforting.

Ingredients

  • 1-2 tbsp vegetable oil or ghee
  • 1 onion, large
  • 4 cloves garlic
  • 1 tbsp grated ginger
  • 1 tbsp ground coriander
  • 1 tbsp ground cumin
  • 1/2 tsp turmeric
  • 1 tsp ground chilli powder or cayenne pepper
  • 700g / 1.5 lbs chicken breast or skinless boneless chicken thighs
  • 1 large potato, peeled and cut into chunks
  • 400g / 14 oz canned chopped tomatoes
  • 250g / 1 cup water
  • Salt to taste
  • 1 tsp garam masala
  • 2 tbsp fresh cilantro / coriander
